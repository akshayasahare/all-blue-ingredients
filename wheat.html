Product Overview

Our premium Indian Wheat is sourced from the fertile plains of India, known for its high protein content, excellent milling quality, and superior grain texture. Carefully cleaned and processed to meet international standards for food manufacturers and millers.

Key Features

  • High protein content (10% - 12%)
  • Excellent milling quality
  • Superior grain texture and color
  • Machine cleaned and sorted
  • Low moisture content
  • Bulk and customized packaging available
