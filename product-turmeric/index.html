Product Overview

Our premium-quality Turmeric (Haldi) is sourced from carefully selected farms in India, renowned for producing turmeric with its distinctive golden-yellow colour, natural aroma and curcumin content.

The product is carefully cleaned, processed and packed according to agreed specifications to meet the requirements of international buyers. We supply Turmeric for food manufacturers, spice brands, distributors, importers and other B2B buyers.

Turmeric is available in whole dried and powder forms, with specifications and packaging options customizable according to buyer requirements.
