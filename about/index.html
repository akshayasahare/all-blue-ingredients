About Us

Know our journey, values, and the people behind All Blue Ingredients

Who We Are

Your Trusted Export Partner

All Blue Ingredients is a premier export company based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. Since our establishment, we have been dedicated to bringing the finest Indian spices and Indian agricultural products to global markets.

Our name reflects our commitment to excellence and global reach - just as the blue ocean connects continents, we connect Indian producers with international buyers. We pride ourselves on maintaining the highest quality standards, ethical business practices, and reliable supply chain management.

Our Mission

To deliver premium quality Indian products to global markets with integrity, reliability, and excellence.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted export partner, recognized for quality and reliability worldwide.

Meet Our Founder

Akshaya Sahare

Founder & CEO

With over 17 years of experience in domestic trade, Akshaya founded All Blue Ingredients with a vision to bring the finest Indian Agricultural products to the global market. His expertise in quality control, supply chain management, and international relations has helped the company build strong partnerships across 3+ countries.

Before founding All Blue Ingredients, Akshaya held leadership positions in corporate companies, where he developed deep insights into global business dynamics. His commitment to ethical business practices and customer satisfaction forms the cornerstone of ABI's values.

"Quality is not just a standard, it's our promise to every client worldwide. We don't just export products; we export India's rich agricultural heritage."
