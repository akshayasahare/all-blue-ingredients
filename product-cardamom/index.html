Cardamom (Elaichi)

Premium Quality Indian Cardamom for Global Markets

⭐ AAA Grade 🌿 Intense Aroma 🚢 Export Quality ✅ Spice Board Certified

Product Description

Our premium quality Cardamom (Elaichi) is sourced from the finest farms in Kerala and Karnataka, India. Known as the "Queen of Spices," our green cardamom offers intense aroma, rich flavor, and bright green color. Hand-sorted and carefully processed to meet international quality standards.

Key Features

  • High essential oil content (4-8%)
  • Bright green color with uniform size
  • Intense aroma and flavor
  • Available in AAA, AGB, and Bold grades
  • Hand-sorted for export quality
  • Free from artificial colors
  • HACCP and ISO certified
  • Low moisture content (10% max)
