Product Overview

Our premium-quality Red Chilli is sourced from the finest farms in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka, India. Renowned for its vibrant red color, high ASTA value, and medium to high pungency, our chilli varieties are carefully processed to meet the exact demands of international buyers.

We supply Red Chilli to food processors, spice brands, distributors, and importers worldwide. Available in whole, crushed, and powder forms, with specifications and packaging customizable according to buyer requirements.

Available Product Forms

Whole Dried Chilli

Crushed Chilli (Flakes)

Chilli Powder

Finely ground chilli powder with consistent color and heat levels, perfect for spice blends, retail packs, and food manufacturing.
