Product Description

Our premium quality Cumin (Jeera) is sourced from the finest farms in Gujarat and Rajasthan, India. Known for its strong aromatic profile, distinct flavor, and high essential oil content. Carefully harvested, cleaned, and processed to meet international quality standards.
