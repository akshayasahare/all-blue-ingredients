Black Pepper

Premium Quality Indian Black Pepper for Global Markets

⭐ Tellicherry Grade 🌿 High Piperine 🚢 Export Quality ✅ Spice Board Certified

Product Description

Our premium quality Black Pepper is sourced from the finest farms in Kerala and Karnataka, India. Known as the "King of Spices," our black pepper offers high piperine content, strong aromatic profile, and bold appearance. Available in Tellicherry and Malabar grades.

Key Features

  • High piperine content (5-7%)
  • Bold, uniform berries
  • Strong aromatic profile
  • Available in Tellicherry and Malabar grades
  • Whole, crushed, and ground forms
  • Machine cleaned and sorted
  • HACCP and ISO certified
  • Low moisture content (12% max)

Specifications
